# Device checks for 0.5.0

On the tablet with the MPK mini Plus plugged in:

1. First start opens LEARN at lesson 1; the badge turns green when the MPK is found.
2. Keys play part 1 (Poly 106, Brass Section); pads play part 4 (Kit), harder is louder,
   PAD BANK B plays pads 9-16.
3. Tap PART 2's card: the keys play the Funky Bass. Tap PADS on another part: the pads play it.
4. Turn K1 while holding a note: the VCF FREQ follows once the knob reaches its value
   (TURN UP / TURN DOWN until then). Change bank: the knobs move to it.
5. Joystick sideways bends up, up or down bends down, and returns to pitch.
6. Preset arrows and the list change the sound; a changed sound shows `*`.
7. Part 4 on the keys: tap pads on screen, they play and their controls show; MPK pads choose
   the pad the knobs shape.
8. MPK Play, Stop and Record show a notice and never silence anything.
9. Leave and reopen the app: every part sounds as it was left, and the lesson is where it was.
10. Unplug and replug the MPK; plug headphones in and out: sound comes back by itself.
